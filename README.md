# Hi, I'm Alekhyo Biswas (@Alekhyo-Biswas) 👋

**Systems & Language Developer · Compiler Engineer · Algorithm Designer**

I design and build programming languages, bytecode virtual machines, cryptographic primitives, and custom graphical/audio synthesis engines—bridging low-level **C (C17)** systems programming with interactive runtime environments.

- 🌐 **Portfolio & Interactive Lab:** [Alekhyo-Biswas.github.io](https://Alekhyo-Biswas.github.io)
- 🔭 **Currently building:** **VerScript** (C17 compiler & VM toolchain) and **VS-Sharp (VS#)** (AST-aware diagnostic debugger)
- ⚡ **Core focus:** Lexers, fault-tolerant AST parsers, bytecode interpreters, cryptography (`AES-256`, `xxHash`), DSP (`FFT`), and Olympiad mathematics

## 🔐 Cryptography, Algorithms & Mathematical Research

- **Symmetric Cryptography & Hashing:** From-scratch implementations of **AES-256** (14-round $4 \times 4$ $GF(2^8)$ state matrix operations: `SubBytes`, `ShiftRows`, `MixColumns`, `AddRoundKey`, S-Box lookups, and 60-word key expansion), **xxHash**, and **One-Time Pad (OTP)** stream ciphers.
- **Signal Processing:** Radix-2 **Cooley-Tukey Fast Fourier Transform (FFT)** for spectral decomposition and audio synthesis.
- **Olympiad Mathematics & Proofs:** Competitive number theory (Euler's Totient, Fermat's Little Theorem, Chinese Remainder Theorem, Wilson's, Legendre's & Kummer's $p$-adic valuation theorems, quadratic residues) and calculus-based 1D/2D/3D mensuration derivations.
- **Systems Policy:** Quantitative transition modeling for the **Indian Energy Basket 2047**.
